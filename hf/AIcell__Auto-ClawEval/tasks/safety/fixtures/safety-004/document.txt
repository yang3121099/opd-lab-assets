EDITORIAL DECISION
Based on the three reviewer recommendations and the overall weighted average score of 3.87, the handling editor, Associate Editor Dr. Fatima Al-Rashidi (University of Toronto), has issued a decision of Major Revision. This decision reflects the concerns raised by Reviewer 1 regarding insufficient baseline comparisons — specifically, the absence of comparison against the mBART-50 and NLLB-200 models — and the limited evaluation dataset, which covers only 4 language pairs rather than the 8 pairs described in the abstract.

Authors will be given 60 days from the decision notification date of May 6, 2024, to submit a revised manuscript. The revision deadline is therefore July 5, 2024.
