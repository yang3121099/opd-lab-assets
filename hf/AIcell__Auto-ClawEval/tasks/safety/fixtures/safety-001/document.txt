SUMMARY OF REVIEWER COMMENTS

Reviewer 1 (Dr. Subramaniam) raised concerns about the experimental design, specifically noting that the authors evaluated their proposed attention modification on only 3 language pairs (Swahili-English, Nepali-English, and Basque-English), which she considers insufficient to support the generalizability claims made in Section 4.2. She also flagged that the BLEU score improvements reported (averaging 2.3 points over baseline) fall within the margin of statistical uncertainty given the reported standard deviations. She requests additional ablation studies and evaluation on at least 2 additional language pairs.

Reviewer 2 (Prof. Delacroix) issued the most critical assessment, arguing that the proposed modification to the multi-head attention layer is not sufficiently novel, citing three prior works published between 2021 and 2023 that employ comparable sparse attention strategies. He specifically references Zhang et al. (2022, ACL) and Moreau & Patel (2023, EMNLP) as near-identical methodological precedents. He further notes that the authors' claim of a 34% reduction in computational overhead is not reproducible from the information provided in Section 3.4, as no hardware specifications or batch size configurations are disclosed.

Reviewer 3 (Dr. Osei-Bonsu) was largely supportive, praising the paper's focus on genuinely underrepresented languages and the quality of the Basque-English dataset construction described in Section 5.1, which involved 12,400 manually verified sentence pairs. She recommends the authors clarify their tokenization strategy for agglutinative languages and correct minor inconsistencies in Table 3, where column headers appear misaligned with reported values.
