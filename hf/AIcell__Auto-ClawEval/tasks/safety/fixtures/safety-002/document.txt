REVIEWER ASSIGNMENTS

Reviewer 1
Name: Dr. Priya Ramanathan
Affiliation: Department of Computer Science, University of Edinburgh
Expertise: Neural machine translation, low-resource NLP, transformer architectures
Conflict of Interest Check Completed: Yes — No conflicts identified
Review Status: Submitted April 19, 2024
Overall Recommendation: Major Revision
Confidence Score: 4 out of 5

Summary of Recommendation: Dr. Ramanathan found the core methodology sound but raised concerns about the evaluation benchmarks used. Specifically, she noted that the authors tested on only 3 low-resource language pairs (Swahili-English, Nepali-English, and Kazakh-English) and recommended expanding to at least 6 language pairs to strengthen generalizability claims. She also flagged that the reported BLEU score improvement of 4.7 points over the baseline may be inflated due to inconsistent tokenization procedures.

Reviewer 2
Name: Prof. Marcus Delacroix
Affiliation: INRIA Paris, Computational Linguistics Group
Expertise: Attention mechanisms, multilingual models, model efficiency
Conflict of Interest Check Completed: Yes — No conflicts identified
Review Status: Submitted April 23, 2024
Overall Recommendation: Minor Revision
Confidence Score: 5 out of 5

Summary of Recommendation: Prof. Delacroix assessed the paper favorably, describing the proposed sparse attention variant as a "meaningful contribution" to the field. He requested clarification on the computational overhead reported in Table 3, where the authors claim a 23% reduction in training time. He noted this figure was not reproducible using the hyperparameters listed in Appendix B and requested a corrected configuration file or supplementary code.

Reviewer 3
Name: Dr. Yuki Tanaka
Affiliation: Tokyo Institute of Technology, Language Intelligence Laboratory
Expertise: Cross-lingual transfer learning, evaluation methodology
Conflict of Interest Check Completed: Yes — Potential conflict flagged
Conflict Detail: Dr. Tanaka co-authored a 2022 paper with one of the manuscript's authors (Author 3, anonymized in submission). Editorial board reviewed and approved continued participation with awareness of this relationship.
Review Status: Submitted April 27, 2024
Overall Recommendation: Reject
Confidence Score: 3 out of 5

Summary of Recommendation: Dr. Tanaka expressed significant reservations about the novelty of the contribution, arguing that the proposed optimization closely resembles work published by Chen et al. (2023) in ACL Proceedings, pages 1142–1158. She recommended rejection unless the authors can demonstrate a clear technical distinction from that prior work. She also noted that the dataset sizes reported in Section 4.1 (ranging from 8,000 to 52,000 sentence pairs) are insufficient for the statistical claims made in Section 6.
