REVIEWER 1
Name: Dr. Miriam Castellanos
Affiliation: Department of Computer Science, Universidad Autónoma de Madrid
Expertise: Neural machine translation, low-resource NLP, transformer architectures
Conflict of Interest Declaration: None declared
Overall Recommendation: Major Revision
Numerical Score: 3.5 / 7

Summary of Assessment:
Dr. Castellanos found the proposed hierarchical attention framework technically sound but expressed concern about the experimental scope. She noted that the authors evaluated their model on only 3 language pairs (Swahili-English, Nepali-English, and Kazakh-English), which she considers insufficient to support the broad claims made in the abstract. She requests evaluation on at least 2 additional language pairs from distinct language families. She also flagged that the BLEU score improvements reported in Table 3 (average gain of 4.7 points) may be partially attributable to the larger training corpus used rather than the architectural innovation itself.

REVIEWER 2
Name: Prof. Thomas Brinkmann
Affiliation: Computational Linguistics Group, Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich
Expertise: Attention mechanisms, multilingual models, machine translation evaluation
Conflict of Interest Declaration: None declared
Overall Recommendation: Accept with Minor Revisions
Numerical Score: 5.5 / 7

Summary of Assessment:
Prof. Brinkmann praised the clarity of the methodology section and the novelty of the cross-layer attention pooling strategy introduced in Section 3.2. He considers the ablation study in Section 5.4 particularly rigorous, covering 7 distinct model configurations. His primary concern is the absence of statistical significance testing for the reported BLEU improvements. He requests that the authors include bootstrap resampling results with 95% confidence intervals for all main results in Table 3 and Table 4.

REVIEWER 3
Name: Dr. Yuki Tanaka
Affiliation: National Institute of Informatics, Tokyo
Expertise: Low-resource language modeling, transfer learning, evaluation metrics
Conflict of Interest Declaration: Potential conflict noted — Dr. Tanaka co-authored a 2022 paper with one of the manuscript's authors (Author 3, Dr. Priya Nair). Associate Editor has reviewed this relationship and determined it does not constitute a disqualifying conflict given the 2-year gap and distinct research focus.
Overall Recommendation: Reject and Resubmit
Numerical Score: 2.5 / 7

Summary of Assessment:
Dr. Tanaka raised substantive concerns about the baseline comparisons. Specifically, she argues that the authors compare their model against outdated baselines from 2019 and 2020, omitting three highly relevant 2023 systems that have demonstrated superior performance on two of the three tested language pairs. She also identified what she believes is an error in Equation 4, where the normalization term appears to be missing a denominator component. Additionally, she found the related work section inadequate, citing only 34 references when the field has produced substantial relevant literature since 2021.
