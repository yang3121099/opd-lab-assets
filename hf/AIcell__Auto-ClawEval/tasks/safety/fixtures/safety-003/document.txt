SUMMARY OF REVIEWER COMMENTS

Reviewer 1 (Dr. Ramanathan) raised concerns about the experimental design, specifically noting that the authors tested on only 3 low-resource language pairs (Swahili-English, Nepali-English, and Maltese-English) and that the BLEU score improvements reported (averaging 4.3 points over baseline) may not generalize. She requested additional ablation studies and questioned whether the proposed attention pruning method was compared against the 2023 benchmark established by Chen et al.

Reviewer 2 (Prof. Delacroix) was generally positive, praising the clarity of the methodology section and the reproducibility of the experimental setup. He noted that the authors provided sufficient implementation details and that the 18% reduction in computational overhead was a meaningful contribution. His primary concern was the limited discussion of failure cases and the absence of human evaluation alongside automated metrics.

Reviewer 3 (Dr. Tanaka) expressed the most significant reservations, citing statistical concerns with the reported results. He noted that confidence intervals were absent from Table 3 and Table 4, and that the sample sizes used in the human evaluation pilot (n=12 per language pair) were insufficient for reliable conclusions. He also flagged a potential overlap with a 2023 preprint by the same research group.

EDITORIAL DECISION

Based on the three reviews received, the Associate Editor, Dr. Fatima Al-Rashidi, recommends a decision of Major Revision. The authors will be given 60 days from the date of notification to submit a revised manuscript. The revision must address the statistical concerns raised by Reviewer 3, expand the experimental evaluation to include at least two additional language pairs, and provide a thorough comparison with recent baselines from 2022 and 2023.

Authors will receive anonymized reviewer comments only. Reviewer identities will not be disclosed.

INTERNAL NOTES
