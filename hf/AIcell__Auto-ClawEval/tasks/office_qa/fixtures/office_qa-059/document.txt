UNITED STATES COMMODITY MARKETS STATISTICAL REPORT
Fiscal Year 2023 Annual Summary
Published by the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) in coordination with the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS)
Release Date: February 14, 2024
Report Reference Number: CFTC-STAT-2024-0214


EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This report presents comprehensive commodity price statistics for fiscal year 2023, covering energy, agricultural, metals, and soft commodity sectors. Data was compiled from exchange-reported transactions on the Chicago Mercantile Exchange (CME), the New York Mercantile Exchange (NYMEX), and the Intercontinental Exchange (ICE), supplemented by import/export price indices maintained by the Bureau of Labor Statistics. The report reflects price movements, volatility indices, year-over-year comparisons, and sector-specific trend analyses for the period January 1, 2023 through December 31, 2023.

Overall, commodity markets experienced significant normalization in 2023 following the extreme volatility of 2021 and 2022. The Bloomberg Commodity Index declined 13.8 percent over the course of 2023, closing the year at 96.4 points compared to 111.8 points at the start of the year. This decline was broad-based but most pronounced in the energy sector.


SECTION 1: ENERGY COMMODITIES

1.1 Crude Oil

West Texas Intermediate (WTI) crude oil opened 2023 at $80.26 per barrel on January 3, 2023, and closed the year at $71.65 per barrel on December 29, 2023, representing a full-year decline of 10.7 percent. The annual average price for WTI crude was $77.58 per barrel, compared to $94.33 per barrel in 2022, a year-over-year decrease of 17.8 percent.

Brent crude oil, the international benchmark, averaged $82.17 per barrel in 2023, down from $101.32 per barrel in 2022, a decline of 18.9 percent. The Brent-WTI spread averaged $4.59 per barrel throughout the year.

The highest WTI price recorded in 2023 was $93.68 per barrel, reached on September 27, 2023, following OPEC+ production cut announcements. The lowest price was $66.74 per barrel, recorded on May 4, 2023, amid concerns over U.S. banking sector instability and weakening Chinese demand signals.

1.2 Natural Gas

Henry Hub natural gas spot prices averaged $2.57 per million British thermal units (MMBtu) in 2023, a dramatic decline of 62.3 percent from the 2022 average of $6.81 per MMBtu. This represented the lowest annual average price for natural gas since 2020. The price peaked at $3.63 per MMBtu on January 12, 2023, and reached a low of $1.73 per MMBtu on March 14, 2023, the lowest single-day price recorded since September 2020.

1.3 Gasoline and Distillates

Retail regular gasoline averaged $3.53 per gallon nationally in 2023, compared to $3.99 per gallon in 2022, a decrease of 11.5 percent. Ultra-low sulfur diesel averaged $4.04 per gallon in 2023, down from $5.11 per gallon in 2022, a decline of 20.9 percent.


SECTION 2: AGRICULTURAL COMMODITIES

2.1 Grains

Corn futures on the CME averaged $4.87 per bushel in 2023, compared to $6.54 per bushel in 2022, a year-over-year decline of 25.5 percent. The December 2023 corn contract closed at $4.73 per bushel on December 29, 2023.

Wheat (CBOT soft red winter) averaged $6.11 per bushel in 2023, down 28.4 percent from the 2022 average of $8.54 per bushel. The sharp decline reflected improved global supply conditions following the disruptions caused by the Russia-Ukraine conflict in 2022.

Soybeans averaged $13.42 per bushel in 2023, compared to $14.98 per bushel in 2022, a decrease of 10.4 percent. Soybean meal, a key livestock feed ingredient, averaged $391.20 per short ton in 2023, up 4.7 percent from $373.60 per short ton in 2022.

2.3 Livestock and Dairy

Live cattle futures averaged $1.6423 per pound in 2023, an increase of 14.2 percent from $1.4381 per pound in 2022. This represented the highest annual average for live cattle since CME records began in their current form. Lean hog futures averaged $0.7891 per pound in 2023, down 9.4 percent from $0.8710 per pound in 2022.

Class III milk futures averaged $16.47 per hundredweight (cwt) in 2023, a significant decline of 31.8 percent from $24.15 per cwt in 2022, reflecting normalization of dairy markets following pandemic-era demand surges.

2.4 Soft Commodities

Arabica coffee futures (ICE) averaged $1.6842 per pound in 2023, compared to $2.0317 per pound in 2022, a decrease of 17.1 percent. Raw sugar No. 11 futures averaged 23.47 cents per pound in 2023, up 23.6 percent from 18.99 cents per pound in 2022, driven by production shortfalls in India and Thailand. Cocoa futures averaged $3,287 per metric ton in 2023, an increase of 66.2 percent from $1,977 per metric ton in 2022, the largest single-year percentage gain among tracked soft commodities.


SECTION 3: METALS

3.1 Precious Metals

Gold futures on COMEX averaged $1,940.54 per troy ounce in 2023, compared to $1,800.12 per troy ounce in 2022, an increase of 7.8 percent. Gold reached its highest closing price of $2,078.40 per troy ounce on December 1, 2023. Silver averaged $23.35 per troy ounce in 2023, essentially flat compared to $21.73 per troy ounce in 2022, a modest increase of 7.5 percent.

Platinum averaged $1,003.20 per troy ounce in 2023, down 6.1 percent from $1,068.40 per troy ounce in 2022. Palladium experienced the most dramatic decline among precious metals, averaging $1,337.80 per troy ounce in 2023, a decrease of 38.4 percent from $2,171.60 per troy ounce in 2022, driven by reduced automotive demand and increased supply from Russian producers.

3.2 Base Metals

Copper futures on COMEX averaged $3.8512 per pound in 2023, compared to $3.9947 per pound in 2022, a modest decline of 3.6 percent. Aluminum (LME) averaged $2,263 per metric ton in 2023, down 17.4 percent from $2,739 per metric ton in 2022. Nickel averaged $21,544 per metric ton in 2023, a sharp decline of 44.9 percent from $39,100 per metric ton in 2022, following the extraordinary market disruption and short squeeze that occurred on the London Metal Exchange in March 2022.

SECTION 4: PRICE VOLATILITY ANALYSIS

The CFTC Commodity Volatility Index (CVI), an internal composite measure tracking 30-day implied volatility across 24 commodity futures contracts, averaged 18.7 in 2023, compared to 27.4 in 2022 and 22.1 in 2021. This 31.8 percent reduction in average volatility reflects the broad normalization of commodity markets as supply chain disruptions from the COVID-19 pandemic and the initial shock of the Russia-Ukraine conflict dissipated.

Energy sector volatility, as measured by the OVX (CBOE Crude Oil Volatility Index), averaged 34.2 in 2023, down from 47.8 in 2022. Agricultural commodity volatility, measured by the CBOT Agricultural Volatility Index, averaged 21.3 in 2023, compared to 29.6 in 2022.

Notable volatility spikes occurred during the following periods:
- March 10-17, 2023: Energy and financial commodity volatility spiked following the collapse of Silicon Valley Bank on March 10, 2023
- September 20-29, 2023: Crude oil volatility increased sharply following OPEC+ production cut announcements
- October 7-14, 2023: Broad commodity market volatility increased following the outbreak of conflict in the Middle East


SECTION 5: IMPORT AND EXPORT PRICE INDICES

According to BLS data, the U.S. Import Price Index for fuels declined 22.4 percent in 2023 on a year-over-year basis. The Import Price Index for agricultural commodities declined 8.7 percent. The Export Price Index for agricultural products declined 11.3 percent, reflecting lower global grain prices.

The Producer Price Index (PPI) for crude goods, which includes raw materials and unprocessed commodities, declined 9.2 percent in 2023, following a 14.6 percent increase in 2022. The PPI for processed goods for intermediate demand declined 4.8 percent in 2023.


SECTION 6: METHODOLOGY AND DATA SOURCES

Price data for futures contracts was sourced from daily settlement prices published by the CME Group, NYMEX, ICE, and the London Metal Exchange (LME). Spot price data for energy commodities was sourced from the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) weekly and daily price reports. Agricultural spot prices were sourced from USDA Agricultural Marketing Service daily reports. All averages represent arithmetic means of daily settlement or spot prices for the full calendar year unless otherwise noted. Year-over-year comparisons use the same methodology for both years being compared.

Currency conversions for LME contracts were performed using daily Federal Reserve H.10 exchange rate data. All prices are denominated in U.S. dollars unless otherwise specified.
