EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This report presents official commodity price statistics for the third quarter of 2024, covering energy, agricultural, metals, and industrial commodities traded in U.S. and international markets. Data has been compiled from the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), the Chicago Mercantile Exchange (CME), the London Metal Exchange (LME), and the U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) National Agricultural Statistics Service. All prices are denominated in U.S. dollars unless otherwise noted.

The third quarter of 2024 demonstrated significant volatility across commodity categories, driven by geopolitical tensions in the Middle East, drought conditions affecting South American agricultural output, and continued adjustments in global monetary policy. The Producer Price Index (PPI) for crude materials rose 3.7 percent on a year-over-year basis as of September 2024, compared to a 1.2 percent increase recorded in the same period of 2023.

SECTION 1: ENERGY COMMODITIES

1.1 Crude Oil

West Texas Intermediate (WTI) crude oil averaged $78.42 per barrel during Q3 2024, representing a 6.3 percent decline from the Q2 2024 average of $83.71 per barrel. Brent crude, the international benchmark, averaged $82.17 per barrel in Q3 2024, down from $87.04 in Q2 2024.

The quarterly low for WTI was recorded on September 10, 2024, at $65.27 per barrel, the lowest settlement price since December 2023. This decline was attributed primarily to weaker-than-expected manufacturing data from China and increased production from non-OPEC+ producers, particularly Brazil and Guyana.

Key WTI Price Points — Q3 2024:
  • July 1, 2024 opening price: $83.38/barrel
  • August 5, 2024 intraday low: $72.94/barrel
  • September 10, 2024 quarterly low: $65.27/barrel
  • September 30, 2024 closing price: $68.17/barrel
  • Q3 2024 average: $78.42/barrel
  • Q3 2023 average: $82.25/barrel (year-over-year change: -4.7%)

1.2 Natural Gas

Henry Hub natural gas spot prices averaged $2.19 per million British thermal units (MMBtu) in Q3 2024, a 14.8 percent decrease from the Q3 2023 average of $2.57/MMBtu. The quarterly high of $2.89/MMBtu was reached on July 19, 2024, coinciding with peak summer cooling demand across the southeastern United States.

U.S. natural gas production reached a record 104.6 billion cubic feet per day (Bcf/d) in August 2024, according to EIA data, contributing to the sustained price suppression observed throughout the quarter.

1.3 Refined Products

  • Retail regular-grade gasoline (national average): $3.31/gallon as of September 30, 2024, down from $3.68/gallon on July 1, 2024
  • Ultra-low sulfur diesel (national average): $3.84/gallon as of September 30, 2024
  • Heating oil (New York Harbor): averaged $2.61/gallon in Q3 2024

SECTION 2: AGRICULTURAL COMMODITIES

2.1 Grains and Oilseeds

Agricultural commodity prices experienced mixed performance in Q3 2024. Corn and wheat prices declined amid favorable harvest conditions in the U.S. Midwest, while soybean prices remained elevated due to reduced output from Argentina, where the 2023–2024 growing season yielded approximately 50.5 million metric tons, down 8.2 percent from the prior year's 55.0 million metric tons.

2.2 Livestock and Dairy

Live cattle futures averaged $183.42 per hundredweight (cwt) in Q3 2024, a 9.7 percent increase from the Q3 2023 average of $167.22/cwt. This price appreciation reflected continued tightness in U.S. cattle supplies, with the January 2024 USDA Cattle Inventory report showing total U.S. cattle and calves at 87.2 million head, the lowest inventory since 1951.

Lean hog futures averaged $84.17/cwt in Q3 2024, compared to $79.43/cwt in Q3 2023, a year-over-year increase of 5.97 percent.

Class III milk futures, which serve as the benchmark for cheese pricing, averaged $19.84 per hundredweight in Q3 2024, up from $15.62/cwt in Q3 2023, representing a 27.0 percent year-over-year increase.

2.3 Soft Commodities

  • Coffee (Arabica, ICE): averaged $2.31/pound in Q3 2024, up 38.9% from $1.66/pound in Q3 2023
  • Cocoa (ICE): averaged $7,842/metric ton in Q3 2024, compared to $3,411/metric ton in Q3 2023, a 129.9% year-over-year increase driven by severe crop failures in Côte d'Ivoire and Ghana
  • Raw sugar (ICE No. 11): averaged 19.84 cents/pound in Q3 2024, down 11.2% from 22.34 cents/pound in Q3 2023
  • Cotton (ICE No. 2): averaged 70.18 cents/pound in Q3 2024

SECTION 3: METALS AND MINERALS

3.1 Precious Metals

Gold prices reached historic levels during Q3 2024. The London Bullion Market Association (LBMA) gold price averaged $2,477.38 per troy ounce in Q3 2024, compared to $1,928.44 per troy ounce in Q3 2023, a year-over-year increase of 28.5 percent. Gold reached an all-time intraday high of $2,531.60 per troy ounce on August 20, 2024.

Silver averaged $29.24 per troy ounce in Q3 2024, up 26.3 percent from $23.15 in Q3 2023. The gold-to-silver ratio averaged 84.7:1 during the quarter.

Platinum averaged $961.42/troy ounce in Q3 2024, while palladium averaged $932.17/troy ounce, the latter representing a 28.4 percent decline from the Q3 2023 average of $1,302.50/troy ounce.

Copper prices were supported by anticipated demand growth from electric vehicle manufacturing and grid infrastructure investment. The International Copper Study Group estimated global refined copper demand at 26.4 million metric tons for full-year 2024, up 2.8 percent from 25.7 million metric tons in 2023.

Nickel continued its multi-year price correction, declining 25.8 percent year-over-year, as Indonesian production capacity expanded significantly. Indonesia produced an estimated 1.8 million metric tons of nickel in 2023, accounting for approximately 54 percent of global mine output.

SECTION 4: COMPOSITE INDICES AND INFLATION METRICS

4.1 Producer Price Index — Crude Materials

The PPI for crude materials for further processing (seasonally adjusted) registered 214.7 in September 2024 (1982=100), compared to 207.1 in September 2023, reflecting a 3.7 percent year-over-year increase. On a month-over-month basis, the index declined 1.2 percent from August 2024 to September 2024, driven primarily by the sharp drop in crude oil prices.

4.2 IMF Primary Commodity Price Index

The International Monetary Fund's Primary Commodity Price Index declined 4.1 percent in Q3 2024 compared to Q2 2024, but remained 1.8 percent above the Q3 2023 level. The energy sub-index fell 7.3 percent quarter-over-quarter, while the food and beverages sub-index rose 2.4 percent over the same period.

4.3 World Bank Commodity Price Index

The World Bank's Pink Sheet data for September 2024 showed the overall commodity price index at 89.4 (2010=100), compared to 91.2 in June 2024 and 87.7 in September 2023.

SECTION 5: OUTLOOK AND RISK FACTORS

The Bureau of Economic Analysis projects continued volatility in commodity markets through Q4 2024. Key risk factors include:

  • OPEC+ production policy decisions scheduled for review at the December 1, 2024 ministerial meeting
  • La Niña weather pattern development, which the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) assessed at 71 percent probability of occurrence during the October 2024–February 2025 period
  • Federal Reserve monetary policy trajectory, with markets pricing in an additional 50 basis points of rate cuts through December 2024 as of the report publication date
  • Ongoing conflict in Ukraine affecting Black Sea grain export volumes, which totaled 3.4 million metric tons in September 2024 under the extended grain corridor arrangements

DATA SOURCES AND METHODOLOGY

Price data for this report was sourced from the following primary databases: EIA Weekly Petroleum Status Report, CME Group DataMine, LME Official Prices, USDA Agricultural Marketing Service, LBMA price statistics, and ICE Futures U.S. All quarterly averages represent arithmetic means of daily settlement or spot prices for the period July 1 through September 30, 2024. Year-over-year comparisons reference the equivalent period July 1 through September 30, 2023.
