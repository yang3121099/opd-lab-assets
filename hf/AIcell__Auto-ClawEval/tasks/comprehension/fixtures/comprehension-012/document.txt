─────────────────────────────────────────────
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
─────────────────────────────────────────────

This report presents findings from a 12-week assessment of Salesforce Sales Cloud Enterprise Edition (v58.0), conducted between June 5 and August 25, 2023, across four business units at Meridian Industrial Supply Corporation. The assessment evaluated 47 documented use cases against current platform capabilities, identified 14 critical gaps, and provides prioritized recommendations for remediation. Total estimated cost of identified gaps, measured in lost productivity and manual workaround labor, is $1.2 million annually.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
1. SCOPE AND METHODOLOGY
─────────────────────────────────────────────

The assessment team consisted of 6 business analysts, 2 Salesforce-certified architects (holding SP-23 and ADM-201 certifications), and 1 data integration specialist. Stakeholder interviews were conducted with 38 employees across the following departments:

- Sales Operations (11 participants)
- Customer Success (9 participants)
- Finance and Revenue Operations (8 participants)
- IT Infrastructure (6 participants)
- Field Service (4 participants)

Data collection methods included:
- Structured interviews using a 94-question protocol
- System log analysis covering January 1 through June 30, 2023
- Process observation sessions totaling 84 hours
- Review of 312 active workflow automations
- Analysis of 6 months of Salesforce adoption metrics from the platform's built-in usage dashboard

The 47 use cases were scored against three criteria: business criticality (1–5), current platform support level (Full, Partial, None), and implementation complexity (Low, Medium, High).

─────────────────────────────────────────────
2. CURRENT PLATFORM UTILIZATION
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Meridian Industrial Supply Corporation has operated Salesforce Sales Cloud since March 2019 and currently holds 214 active user licenses at a contracted rate of $150 per user per month, representing an annual platform spend of $385,200. The organization also maintains 3 add-on licenses for Salesforce CPQ (Configure, Price, Quote) at $75 per user per month.

Platform adoption metrics as of August 2023:

Metric                              Value
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Monthly Active Users                178 of 214 (83.2%)
Daily Login Rate                    61.4%
Average Session Duration            22 minutes
Records Created (Jan–Jun 2023)      148,320
Workflow Automation Runs/Month      94,700
Reports Generated/Month             3,840
Mobile App Usage                    18.7% of sessions

The 83.2% monthly active user rate exceeds the industry benchmark of 74% reported in Salesforce's 2023 State of CRM Adoption Study, suggesting reasonable platform engagement. However, the daily login rate of 61.4% falls below the 70% threshold recommended by Salesforce Success Plans for organizations of comparable size.

3.1 Fully Supported Use Cases (Selected Examples)

The following high-priority use cases are functioning as intended with no remediation required:

- Lead-to-opportunity conversion tracking with automated stage progression
- Territory-based account assignment using Salesforce Territory Management 2.0
- Opportunity pipeline reporting with real-time dashboards
- Email integration via Salesforce Inbox for Microsoft Outlook 365
- Contract lifecycle management using standard Salesforce Contracts object

3.2 Partially Supported Use Cases

Eighteen use cases are receiving partial platform support, meaning core functionality exists but requires significant manual intervention or workarounds. The five highest-impact partial gaps are:

USE CASE: Multi-currency quoting for international accounts
Current State: Sales reps manually convert prices using a shared Excel spreadsheet (file last updated April 3, 2023) before entering quotes into Salesforce CPQ. This process averages 47 minutes per quote.
Gap: Salesforce CPQ's Advanced Currency Management feature is licensed but not configured. Estimated annual labor cost of workaround: $84,000 (based on 2,400 quotes per year at average rep salary of $52,000).

USE CASE: Field service scheduling integration
Current State: Field service technicians use a separate system, ServiceMax version 19.2, which has no live data connection to Salesforce. Dispatchers manually re-enter job completion data into Salesforce within 24–48 hours.
Gap: A Salesforce Field Service license package was quoted at $165 per user per month for 22 technicians but has not been procured. Data latency causes 12% of customer follow-up calls to reference outdated service records.

USE CASE: Renewal opportunity automation
Current State: Customer Success Managers manually review contract end dates in a shared Google Sheet and create renewal opportunities in Salesforce. The sheet contains 1,847 active contracts.
Gap: Salesforce's standard contract renewal automation requires a custom Apex trigger or the use of Revenue Cloud. Neither has been implemented. Estimated 6 renewal opportunities missed per quarter due to manual process failures, representing approximately $210,000 in annual recurring revenue at risk.

USE CASE: Partner portal self-service order entry
Current State: 43 active reseller partners submit orders via email to an internal inbox (orders@meridianindustrial.com), which is monitored by 2 full-time employees.
Gap: Salesforce Experience Cloud (formerly Community Cloud) is not licensed. A Partner Community license costs $10 per login or $35 per member per month. Estimated ROI of implementation: 14 months based on labor savings of $128,000 annually.

USE CASE: Real-time inventory availability in opportunity records
Current State: Sales reps call or email the warehouse team to confirm stock before committing delivery dates. Average response time is 3.2 hours during peak periods (October–December).
Gap: No integration exists between Salesforce and the company's ERP system, SAP S/4HANA (version 2021). An MuleSoft Anypoint Platform connector is available but requires a separate license estimated at $60,000 per year.

3.3 Unsupported Use Cases

Eight use cases have no current platform support. The three most critical are:

1. AI-driven lead scoring: Sales leadership requested predictive lead scoring in Q1 2023. Salesforce Einstein Lead Scoring is available within the current Enterprise Edition license but has not been activated. Configuration requires approximately 40 hours of administrator time.

2. Automated revenue recognition reporting: Finance requires ASC 606-compliant revenue recognition schedules. Salesforce Revenue Cloud (formerly Billing) would address this but carries an estimated implementation cost of $95,000 based on a proposal received from Silverline CRM on July 18, 2023.

3. Customer health scoring dashboard: Customer Success lacks a consolidated view of account health. Gainsight CS, which integrates with Salesforce, was evaluated in May 2023 at a cost of $30,000 per year for the current user base.

5. PRIORITIZED RECOMMENDATIONS
─────────────────────────────────────────────

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS (0–30 days, no additional cost):
- Activate Salesforce Einstein Lead Scoring using existing Enterprise Edition entitlement. Estimated configuration time: 40 hours. Projected improvement in lead conversion rate: 8–12% based on Salesforce benchmark data from comparable manufacturing distributors.
- Configure Advanced Currency Management within existing CPQ license. Estimated configuration time: 16 hours. Eliminates $84,000 in annual workaround labor.

SHORT-TERM ACTIONS (30–90 days, budget required):
- Procure Salesforce Experience Cloud Partner Community licenses for 43 partners. Recommended licensing model: $35 per member per month ($18,060 annually). Payback period: 14 months.
- Engage internal Apex developer or contract resource to build renewal opportunity automation. Estimated development cost: $12,000–$18,000 at current contractor rates of $150 per hour.

LONG-TERM ACTIONS (90–180 days, significant investment):
- Issue RFP for MuleSoft SAP integration by October 31, 2023. Three vendors should be invited: Deloitte Digital, Slalom, and Accenture's Salesforce practice.
- Evaluate Salesforce Revenue Cloud implementation with Silverline CRM. Decision required by November 15, 2023 to align with FY2024 budget cycle.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
6. CONCLUSION
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Meridian Industrial Supply Corporation has established a functional Salesforce foundation with 83.2% monthly active user adoption and 312 active automations. However, 14 identified gaps are collectively costing an estimated $1.2 million annually in labor inefficiency and revenue risk. Seven of the 14 gaps can be addressed within the existing license entitlement or at minimal incremental cost. The highest-priority action is activating Einstein Lead Scoring and configuring Advanced Currency Management within 30 days, as both require no additional spend and address documented pain points raised by 22 of 38 interview participants.

A follow-up assessment is recommended for March 2024 to measure progress against this baseline.
